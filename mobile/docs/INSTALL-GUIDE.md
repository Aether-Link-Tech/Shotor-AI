# Shotor AI: install guide (Android)

*Shotor AI by Aether Link*

Shotor AI is your private assistant. It summarizes reports, answers questions, drafts email replies and reminds you of meetings. The AI runs **on your phone**, so your documents and messages never leave it.

## What you need
- An Android phone, Android 8 or newer. **6 GB of RAM or more** is recommended.
- About **3 GB of free storage**.
- Wi-Fi for the one-time AI download.

## 1. Install the app
1. Open the file **`ShotorAI.apk`** you received (from WhatsApp, email, Drive or Files).
2. If Android says *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*, tap **Settings**, switch on **Allow from this source**, then go back.
3. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.

If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap **More details → Install anyway**. This is normal for apps that don't come from the Play Store.

## 2. First-time setup (2 minutes)
The welcome screen walks you through it:
1. **Your name**: used in greetings and to sign email drafts.
2. **Allow access**: tap it and choose **Allow** for *Calendar* and *Notifications*. This lets the app show today's meetings and remind you before each one.
3. **Offline AI model**: tap **Download AI model**. It is about 2 GB, so stay on Wi-Fi and keep the app open. It takes roughly 5–15 minutes. After this the AI works **without internet**.
4. **App lock** (recommended): asks for your fingerprint or face whenever the app opens.
5. Tap **Start using Shotor**.

## 3. Everyday use
| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Your daily brief, today's meetings, unread mail and anything waiting to send |
| **Reports** | Tap **New report**, choose a PDF, Word, PowerPoint or Excel file (or paste text), and get a short summary with key numbers, risks and decisions needed |
| **Ask AI** | Ask questions such as *"What do I need to decide today?"* or *"What were the risks in the Q3 report?"* |
| **Mail** | Connect Gmail once. Open an email and tap **Draft reply with AI**, edit it, then **Approve & send**. If you are offline, the reply waits in the Outbox and sends by itself when you are back online. |
| **Settings** | Reminder time, daily brief time, AI model, app lock, erase data |

## Good to know
- The AI writes on your phone, so a long report can take a minute or two. Keep the app open while it works.
- **Nothing is ever sent without your approval.** Always read AI drafts before sending.
- Updates: install the new `ShotorAI.apk` the same way. Your data stays.
- To remove everything, use **Settings → Erase all data on this phone**, or uninstall the app.
