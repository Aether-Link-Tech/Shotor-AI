Delete your Shotor AI account

You can delete your Shotor account from the Android app under Settings → Delete account. The app asks you to confirm your password and then removes your account, saved documents and summaries, saved email drafts, Shotor sessions, and stored Google connection.

If you cannot access the app

Contact the Shotor service operator using the support address published in the app listing or the operator's official website. Include the account email address and request account deletion. Do not send your password, Google tokens, or other credentials. The operator should verify account ownership before processing the request.

Data retention

Account data stored by Shotor is intended to be deleted when the request is completed. External Google token revocation is attempted but can fail if Google is unavailable; removing the stored token prevents Shotor from using it. Google may retain its own records according to Google's policies. Operators must publish a real support contact and confirm their actual retention practices before publishing this page.
